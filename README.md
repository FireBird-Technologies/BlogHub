## Great writing shouldn't stay invisible

You write every week. You hit publish. Then silence.

Substack is a wonderful place to write and a hard place to be found. BlogHub fixes that. It's a free directory where Substackers, newsletter authors, and bloggers launch their publication in front of readers who are actively looking for something new to love.

No listing fee. No subscriber minimum. Just a public home for your work and a link straight back to it.

## Launch your newsletter like a product

- **Your own launch page.** Every publication gets a beautiful, searchable page with your pitch, your category, and a direct link to subscribe.
- **Upvotes that mean something.** Readers vote for the newsletters they love. Real support, visible to everyone.
- **Conversations around your writing.** Comments turn a listing into a community.
- **Rankings built for your niche.** Compete in your category, not against the whole internet. Rise to the top of your corner.
- **Monthly Top Blogs.** The best publications of the month, celebrated in one place and shared with thousands of readers.
- **The Underrated list.** Small newsletter? Big talent? We spotlight the hidden gems readers haven't found yet.
- **Verified by you.** Claim your listing and wear the verified badge.

## Turn visitors into subscribers

BlogHub brings around 10,000 visitors to a library of publications they're eager to explore. Want a bigger moment? **Feature your newsletter** and take the spotlight, front and center.

Every listing is a backlink, a discovery channel, and a signal that your writing is worth reading.

## Who it's for

- **New Substackers** chasing their first 100 subscribers
- **Growing writers** ready for their next 1,000
- **Established voices** who want a steady stream of new readers
- **Curious readers** who love finding their next favorite newsletter

## Get discovered in four steps

1. **Submit** your publication. It's free.
2. **Rally** your readers to upvote and comment.
3. **Climb** your category and land in the monthly roundup.
4. **Feature** your newsletter when you want a big push.
